# A03: Make the last seconds readable

Started: 2026-10-20 22:52 UTC. Baseline: 12898796 (Gems Together 3.3.9).

Actual self-play completed a full two-minute challenge through normal pointer
input: 59 moves, 32,300 points, 296 gems and a six-wave best cascade. The small
white seconds label faded into bright scenery and fireworks. The final accepted
cascade continued for about three seconds after expiry, without explaining why
the result had not yet appeared.

A03 gives the timed challenge a dark backing, a remaining-time bar and a clock
label. The last ten seconds use a static amber FINAL label; expiry reads TIME UP
and Finishing cascade while the accepted turn settles. The display respects the
existing host clock, scoring and end-of-run behavior. It adds no flashing,
sounds, saved fields, room packets, controls or challenge rules. Personal ghost
comparisons and all other modes retain their existing behavior.

The first capture pass exposed control-rail overlap at the shortest landscape
sizes. The countdown now uses a left-side card when there is no sidebar and
compacts its backing on short sidebars. Fresh captures verify both fixes. The
expiry fixture also revealed that its nine-wave cascade takes over 16 seconds;
the test waits for completion within a bounded minute and checks the actual
finished reason, final score and move count.
