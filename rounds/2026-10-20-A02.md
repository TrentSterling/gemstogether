# A02: Give each finished challenge a replay target

Started: 2026-10-20 22:00 UTC. Baseline: e5397b48 (Gems Together 3.3.8).

Actual self-play completed a 30-move run through normal pointer input: 18,200
points, 211 gems and a three-wave best cascade. The result screen showed the
score and cascade but no indication that a first record had been saved or what
score to chase on replay. A02 adds a compact gold or mint comparison plaque
above the existing replay buttons: first score, new best, tied best or gap to
the player's best. Daily runs compare only records from the same daily board.

The comparison is captured before the existing best-score save and remains
local presentation. Co-op partners retain separate records. No new save keys,
profile fields, room packets, scoring rules, controls or challenge budgets are
introduced. Puzzles keep their existing result actions. The bright arcade
identity and the official Gems Together name are retained.

Independent review identified and corrected overlap on short landscape screens
and ambiguous "today" wording for runs completed after midnight. Result text
now compacts above full-size replay buttons, and daily labels refer to the
daily board. Final screenshots and release gates are retained with the receipt.
