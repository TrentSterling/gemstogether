# Egyptian cabinet and earned solar feedback — unpublished candidate

Local version `3.4.0-candidate.1` extends draft PR26 at
`81e08395f3f3c2b832f3e75f69eee0098024cf15`. The existing 3.3.30 notice
placement changes are preserved. Public main remains 3.3.29 at
`e9052758421e8bd1d149e463e85c0404fd6e475a` when this work began.

The active public source had bronze/cyan garden architecture but no explicit
Egyptian relief layer. The new authored geometry adds a faceted scarab with
gold-and-turquoise feather wings, lapis inlay, carved ankhs and lotus caps,
stepped courtyard obelisks, and a collar beneath the title. The existing gem
silhouettes and palette, garden, stage identities and controls remain intact.

Each accepted match opens the carved wings and sends a finite chase along
mounted channels. At x3, two rail seals light up; x4 charges the collar, raised wing spine or
compact rail inlays; x5 opens a solar fan behind the cabinet; x7 adds an
interlocking turquoise halo. Raised portrait ornaments have a fitted local
solar disk, camera-impulse clearance and a measured lane around About.
The new layer has no particle emission or screen flash, no RNG consumption,
and no audio calls. Five persistent mesh batches cap at 1 wing, 1 optional carved staff, 24 short
lights, 2 seals and 4 suns. At most three event records are retained.

The layer reuses the existing accepted-flash result and honors Motion,
Particles, Combo Treatment, Flash Intensity and Juice. Low attenuates the full
envelope to 35%; Off/reduced motion suppress transient geometry and retain
the quiet carvings. Panels, results, photos and AA comparison clear pending
events, preventing a deferred burst when returning. New Board resets it.

Build with `python tools/polish.py`. It reads `tools/egyptian-art.js` and uses
narrow exact-count anchors; the frozen original drop is unchanged. Neither
board simulation nor shader, renderer, save, progression, network, audio or
PR26 notice source is modified.

CPU/source validation includes exact unrelated-runtime reconstruction,
geometry normals, ten viewport projections, 6,336 preference/time samples,
10,000 repeated events, bounded allocations and lifecycle suppression.
Independent review corrected clipped crest tips, offscreen phone seals and
the depth orientation of the physical light chase. Actual visual/backend
inspection and costs are recorded in the companion candidate evidence.

This is not a release. `python run-gems-gates.py` was denied before execution
because its swap and co-op checks simulate mouse/right-click input. Explicit
clarification remains pending. The art request does not authorize those
actions, and presentation-only checks do not replace the required Chrome,
native Firefox, touch, host/peer, exact-commit CI and live verification gates.
No retry, weakened check, remote push, PR promotion, merge or deployment is
part of this candidate.
